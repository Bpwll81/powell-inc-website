Privacy Policy for Powell Global LLC

Applies to Agent 76, Varis Sniper, Varis and Varis Prelude.
Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Plain-English summaries are reading aids, not replacements for the full policy text below each summary.

1. Introduction

Plain English: One policy covers our game across all its names.

Powell Global LLC ("we," "us," or "our") develops and publishes the mobile game Agent 76 (including all previous, current, and future releases titled Varis Sniper, Varis, and Varis Prelude, collectively referred to herein as the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you play our App, specifically detailing your rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA.

2. Information We Collect

Plain English: No account is required. Service providers collect device, advertising and gameplay information.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identifying information (such as your name or email address) to play the App. However, to support core gameplay, save states, and monetization, our third-party service providers automatically collect the following data:

  • Device Information: Operating system version, hardware model, device language, and time zone.
  • Identifiers: Mobile advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Apple IDFA) and unique device identifiers.
  • Network Data: IP addresses and network connection state.
  • Usage Data: In-game interactions, session length, and level completion status.

3. How We Use Your Information

Plain English: Data supports gameplay, ads, rewards, performance and fraud prevention.

The data collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Delivering, maintaining, and improving the App's functionality.
  • Serving personalized and non-personalized advertisements.
  • Providing in-game rewards via third-party offerwalls.
  • Analyzing game performance, diagnosing crashes, and preventing fraud.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

Plain English: Google, Unity and Tapjoy have their own data practices. Read their policies here.

Our App uses third-party monetization and mediation networks. These partners act as independent data controllers or service providers and collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies. Our current partners include:

  • Google AdMob privacy policy
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads & LevelPlay) privacy policy
  • Tapjoy (Offerwall) privacy policy

5. EU and UK Privacy Rights (GDPR)

Plain English: You can change your ad consent and ask to access, correct or delete your data.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are protected by the GDPR and UK GDPR.

  • Consent Management: Upon your first launch of the App, you will be presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. We rely on your explicit consent as the legal basis to process your data for personalized advertising.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, or object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Revoking Consent: You may revoke or modify your advertising consent at any time by navigating to the "Privacy Options" or "Settings" menu within the App, or by utilizing the data request form hosted on our website.

6. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Plain English: Eligible US residents can request access or deletion and opt out of targeted-ad sharing.

If you are a resident of California or a state with similar comprehensive privacy laws, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know and Access: You have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of data collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of "Sale" or "Sharing": We do not sell your data for direct monetary profit. However, sharing advertising IDs with third-party ad networks to serve targeted ads qualifies as a "sale" or "sharing" under California law.
  • How to Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising globally by adjusting your device-level settings:
    • Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads > and select "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID."

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Plain English: The App is not directed at children. Contact us if a child has provided personal data.

Our App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 in the United States, or under the age of 16 in the EEA/UK. If we discover that a child under these age thresholds has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data, please contact us.

8. Data Retention and Security

Plain English: Data is retained for necessary purposes or legal obligations, with security protections used by our partners.

Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal obligations. Our third-party partners utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect data during transit and storage.
