Macro Rationale: The Copper Floor

Copper is the fundamental metal of infrastructure. We maintain a 5% allocation to ensure Unity is tethered to the physical electrification of the global grid. UNT's dedicated secondary-metals bedrock comprises Silver, Platinum, Palladium, and Copper, separate from UGLD's gold-only backing. UNT has a 70,000,000,000 genesis supply and a 35,000,000,000 target across the 25-year halving epoch. At Month 80, Copper caps at 5,000,000,000 grams and 100% of overflow redirects to the UNT Null Address burn.

Regulatory & Tax Management

Physical Copper acquisition can trigger VAT or Sales Tax liabilities. Powell Global LLC manages these complexities at the treasury level by capping the allocation at 5.00%, ensuring UNT reserve management remains separate from UGLD's gold-only backing.
